At a Thai-Isan Wedding: Before you go
Welcome to an Isan Wedding Morning

Welcome to an Isan wedding. In the northeast, a wedding is traditionally called kin dong, a celebration that binds two families as relatives. Thai ceremonies follow a roek, an auspicious moment often chosen with a monk or astrologer's help. Many Thais consider nine lucky, because in Thai it sounds like the word for stepping forward. The dress theme is Isan traditional dress. The region is known for handwoven silk such as mudmee, whose threads are tied and dyed before weaving. A sin is a woman's long skirt, usually reaching the ankle, and a pha khao ma is a checked cotton cloth often tied around the waist. Avoid black, the colour of funerals. Greet elders with a wai, palms together and head slightly bowed. Every family does things differently.
