At a Thai-Isan Wedding: Stop 2
The Monk Ceremony

The morning starts with monks, invited to chant blessings for the couple. In the form described by Thailand's Department of Religious Affairs, the bride and groom sit before a Buddha altar, the bride on the groom's left. Together they light candles and incense, bow, and receive the precepts from the monks, along with everyone present. You may see a white cotton cord, the sai sin, which monks hold while they chant. During the chanting, the couple put food into the monks' alms bowls side by side, then offer a meal and gifts. Afterwards they slowly pour water from a small vessel, the kruat nam, to share the merit, and the monks sprinkle holy water, on the couple first, then often on others. Be calm and gentle around monks, in manner and in words. Women should never touch a monk.
