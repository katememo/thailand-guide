Kham Chanot: Stop 1
An Island in the Rice Fields

Welcome to Kham Chanot. Look out across the flat rice fields of Ban Dung district and you will see a patch of forest that looks like an island in the middle of the paddies. That is where we are heading. The forest lies beside a temple called Wat Siri Suttho, in Ban Muang subdistrict, roughly a hundred kilometres from the city of Udon Thani. Many people also know it by another name, Wang Nakhin. Thai tourism authorities describe Kham Chanot as a sacred place, deeply respected by the people of the surrounding villages, who come to pay their respects in an unbroken stream. Entry is free. As you walk, remember that for many of the people around you, this is a place of living faith.
