At a Thai-Isan Wedding: Part 5
The Luncheon

At noon, the morning ends the Isan way, with a shared meal. Kin means to eat, and dong refers to a couple sharing a life, so kin dong is the feast that celebrates a wedding. Traditionally, relatives and neighbours gather at the bride's home the day before to prepare, and the hosts thank them with a meal called muea tao. Guests traditionally share dishes such as laap, finely chopped meat or fish with lime and chilli, often with music, sometimes mor lam, the singers of the northeast. The feast is also a chance for the couple to meet the elders of both families, making one larger family. Today's luncheon is different: it is served Western style, with a four-course Isan fusion menu, and each guest receives the dishes they selected. Enjoy it, thank your hosts, and congratulate the couple.
