At a Thai-Isan Wedding: Stop 6
The Feast, Kin Dong

At noon, the morning ends the Isan way, with a shared meal. In Thai, kin dong also simply means the wedding feast. Traditionally, guests share local dishes such as laap, finely chopped meat or fish seasoned with lime and chilli, often with music, sometimes mor lam, the singers of the northeast. In a village, neighbours usually come together to cook and prepare, so the feast gathers the whole community. It is also a chance for the couple to meet the elders of both families, making one larger family. Today's lunch is an Isan fusion menu in four courses. Enjoy it, thank your hosts, and take a moment to congratulate the couple before you leave. Thank you for sharing this day with them.
