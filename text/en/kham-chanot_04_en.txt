Kham Chanot: Stop 4
The Shrine of Grandfather Si Suttho

Deeper inside the forest island stands the shrine of Pu Si Suttho, which means Grandfather Si Suttho. The legend says that the naga realm beneath Kham Chanot is ruled and protected by the naga king Pu Si Suttho, together with Ya Si Pathumma, Grandmother Si Pathumma, who is honoured as a naga queen. In the stories, Si Suttho is described as a great naga of the Mekong River basin. Most people who come to pray here ask for wealth, plenty and good fortune, and the Tourism Authority of Thailand notes that the site is especially known for prayers for luck and for wishes coming true. You are welcome to watch quietly, and to give those who are praying the space and calm they came for.
