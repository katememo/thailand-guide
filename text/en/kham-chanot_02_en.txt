Kham Chanot: Stop 2
The Naga Walkway

Before you enter the forest, you come to a concrete walkway. Look at the walls on either side. They are shaped as the long bodies of nagas, the great serpents of legend, and they lead you in toward the trees. This is where everyone takes off their shoes and walks barefoot, so slip yours off before you set out along the path. And why nagas? According to a folk legend of the Isan and Lao peoples, this forest is the mouth of an underwater city, a gateway to the realm where the nagas live. For the believers walking beside you, this path leads not just into a forest, but toward the edge of that hidden world. Take your time on the walkway.
