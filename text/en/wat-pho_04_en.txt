Wat Pho: Stop 4
The Great Chedis of Four Kings

Inside the white wall rise four great chedis, each forty-two metres high and decorated with glazed tiles. Together they are known as Phra Maha Chedi Si Rajakarn, the great chedis of the four reigns. The green chedi dates from King Rama the First. It was built to enclose Phra Si Sanphet, a ruined standing Buddha sixteen metres tall, brought from the royal palace of the old capital, Ayutthaya, and relics of the Buddha are kept inside. King Rama the Third added the white chedi in honour of his father, King Rama the Second, and the yellow one for his own reign. The dark blue chedi belongs to King Mongkut, Rama the Fourth, who modelled it on a chedi from the Ayutthaya period. He then had all four enclosed within this wall, leaving no room for any more.
