The Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew: Stop 5
Robes for Three Seasons

The Emerald Buddha is dressed in golden robes that change with the seasons: one set for summer, one for the rainy season and one for winter. King Rama the First dedicated the summer and rainy season robes, and King Rama the Third added the winter set. They are crafted from gold and adorned with diamonds and precious stones. Three times a year, on set days of the lunar calendar, the king changes the robes in a royal ceremony. In the past, blessed water from the ceremony was sprinkled only on the princes and officials inside the hall, but in King Bhumibol's reign the king also blessed the people waiting around the hall. The old robes, masterpieces of craftsmanship, had worn with use, so they are now conserved, and a new set was made for the king's Golden Jubilee in 1996.
