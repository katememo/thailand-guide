Before you go: Stop 1
Temple manners

Welcome to your guide to Thailand! Before you explore its temples and sacred places, here are some temple manners. The Grand Palace is a place of reverence, so dress with care. No sleeveless or see-through tops, short trousers, mini skirts, or torn or tight trousers. Wat Pho also asks for polite dress and no shorts. There, take off your shoes and leave them on the shelf before entering a religious building. Stay calm and polite, and be gentle with monks. Women must not touch monks or novices. Respect every Buddha image, never step on one, and tuck your feet away when sitting. At the palace, do not touch murals or statues. Photos are fine around the Temple of the Emerald Buddha, but not inside its ordination hall. Enjoy!

[Not confirmed by a saved source] When sitting, tuck your feet away so they do not point at a Buddha image.
