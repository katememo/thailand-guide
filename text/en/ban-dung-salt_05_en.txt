Ban Dung Salt Fields: Stop 5
Visiting the Salt Pits

Come in the dry season if you can. Sun-dried salt needs rainless weather, and a field owner told Khaosod his season runs from November to April. Boiling sheds, researchers say, can work all year. The Ban Dung subdistrict council recommends the iodised salt makers' group in village nine, Ban Dung Nuea, and a 2021 study found the group selling one-kilogram bags for about ten baht, with salt also sold along the roadsides. For a soak, Krungthep Turakij describes the Kunnaphat salt spa in village one of Ban Dung subdistrict, where the owner says sun-warmed brine tops fifty degrees and stays hot for hours. Keep plans flexible. Komchadluek reported in 2021 that salt making here has shrunk a lot, after salty soil spread into rice fields. No official source lists hours or prices.
