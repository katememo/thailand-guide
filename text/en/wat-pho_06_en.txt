Wat Pho: Stop 6
The Ordination Hall and Its Buddha

Walk through the double cloister, lined with hundreds of gilded Buddha images, into the ordination hall, the Phra Ubosot, where monks perform their rituals. King Rama the First built it in the Ayutthaya style, and King Rama the Third rebuilt and enlarged it. Before going in, look at the doors: their outer panels are inlaid with mother-of-pearl scenes from the Ramakien, the Thai Ramayana. Around the outer balustrades, more Ramakien scenes are carved in stone relief, each with its own verses. Inside sits Phra Buddha Theva Patimakorn, in meditation on a three-tiered pedestal. King Rama the First brought this image from another temple and gave it its name. King Mongkut later had some of King Rama the First's ashes placed beneath the pedestal, and since his visit in 1851, kings have come here to pay their respects.
