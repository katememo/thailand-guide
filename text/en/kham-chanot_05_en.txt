Kham Chanot: Stop 5
The Sacred Well

Also inside the forest island, at its very heart, is a sacred well, which some official descriptions call a sacred pond. Believers hold that this water is the point where the underwater city of the nagas meets the world of humans. That belief inspires deep devotion, and many people feel they should come here at least once in their lives, to bow, to worship, and to ask for blessings. Take a quiet look at the water. One request from the official guidance belongs right here. Please do not throw coins into the sacred well, however natural it may feel to make a wish that way. If you would like to mark the moment, a quiet pause is all that is needed.
