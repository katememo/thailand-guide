Ban Dung Salt Fields: Stop 1
Welcome to Ban Dung's Salt Country

Welcome to Ban Dung, the salt country of Udon Thani. Thai media call this area Bo Kluea Ban Dung, the salt pits of Ban Dung, and in 2020 the business daily Krungthep Turakij reported the province promoting it as a new attraction. Salt has deep roots here. The provincial culture office records that the village of Ban Tha Bo Yang took part of its name from ponds where people once went down to make salt, and the local council's motto still praises Dong Na Kluea, a land of salt fields. The same newspaper counts about three thousand rai, close to five square kilometres, of salt land in four clusters around the district, among them Ban Thung and Ban Dung Nuea. The product is kluea sinthao, inland salt, made from underground brine rather than seawater.
