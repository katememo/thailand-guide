Kham Chanot: Stop 6
Visiting with Respect

Before you leave, here are the requests that the Tourism Authority of Thailand passes on to everyone who visits Kham Chanot. Please do not light incense sticks or candles anywhere in the forest. If you bring offerings, including the ceremonial offerings called bai si, take them all back with you when you go. Do not release fish into the waters around the forest. Do not tie seven coloured cloths to anything here, and do not rub powder onto the trees or rub at them in the hope of finding lucky lottery numbers. Opening hours differ between official sources, so check locally before you plan your day. Following these simple requests helps keep this forest calm and sacred for the many people who come here to pray.
